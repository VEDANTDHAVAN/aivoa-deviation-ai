Subject: Deviation Report – Out-of-Range pH During API Manufacturing
Site: Hyderabad Manufacturing Facility
Date of Occurrence: 24 September 2026
Product/Material: API-207
Batch/Lot Number: API207-240926
Source: Batch Manufacturing Record  
During the final processing stage of API-207 batch API207-240926, the process solution pH was observed at 6.8, outside the approved operating range of 7.2–7.6.
The deviation was identified during routine in-process testing. The operator immediately notified the production supervisor and stopped further processing. The pH meter calibration status was verified and found to be within calibration requirements. The batch was placed on hold pending Quality Assurance investigation.
Approximately 1200 L of material was affected. No abnormal appearance was observed during the initial inspection. The process records, analytical results, and equipment history are being reviewed to determine the cause and potential impact on product quality.
Potential impact: The pH excursion may affect process performance and product quality attributes. Further investigation and analytical evaluation are required before batch disposition.
